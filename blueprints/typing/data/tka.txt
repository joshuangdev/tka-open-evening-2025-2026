The Kingston Academy, rated outstanding by Ofsted in both 2018 and 2024, is praised in the reports for being an exceptional school with an ambitious curriculum and exemplary student behaviour. Ofsted is the UK government department that inspects and regulates educational services in England.
The Kingston Academy provides a wide range of GCSE and BTEC options to cater to diverse interests. Besides from regular subjects, students can select from subjects like sociology, media studies, business studies, hospitality and catering, and construction.
